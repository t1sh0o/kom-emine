## Notes

- Seg 11 (Dobrila→Botev shelter): no vehicle access, crew must hike to exchange point
- Seg 12 includes Botev Peak (2376m) - hardest segment, exposed alpine, weather-dependent
- ~12h will be in darkness - headlamps + GPS mandatory
- Seg 22 (Sini Vir) pace of 17.3 km/h is aggressive, may need time borrowed from easier neighbors
- All paces assume fresh, well-rested runners doing a single segment
